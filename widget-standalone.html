📖 What is PCBot?

PCBot is an AI-powered assistant designed to provide instant, accurate answers about Pakistan's development project procedures, based on the official Manual for Development Projects 2024.

💬 How to Use

  • Click the chat button in the bottom-right corner
  • Ask questions about project approval, PC-I forms, DDWP/CDWP/ECNEC
  • Get instant answers with page references from the Manual
  • Rate responses to help improve accuracy

🎯 What Can You Ask?

  • DDWP/CDWP/ECNEC approval limits
  • PC-I, PC-II, PC-III requirements
  • Project approval procedures
  • Cost escalation and revision processes
  • Monitoring & evaluation guidelines
